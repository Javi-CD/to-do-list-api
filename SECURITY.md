# Security policy

The safety of our users and collaborators is a priority. If you find a security vulnerability in this project, we ask you to follow the responsible dissemination process described below.

## How to report vulnerability

- **Do not disseminate vulnerability.**
  Avoid creating public ISSUES, Pull Reques or any other visible channel to report security problems. -**Send a Private Report**Through email to [javierperezdeveloper@gmail.com](mailto:<javierperezdeveloper@gmail.com>).
  Please include in your report:
  - Clear and detailed description of vulnerability.
  - Steps to reproduce the problem.
  - Potential impact and possible solutions if you know them.
  - Any additional information that helps research and solve the problem.

## Answer process

- We will confirm the reception of your report within 72 hours.
- We will investigate the problem and work in a solution as soon as possible.
- We will keep you informed about the progress and the state of correction.
- Once the vulnerability is resolved, we will notify the affected users and publish an update if necessary.
- We will recognize your contribution in project credits, if you wish.

## Scope

This policy applies to any vulnerability that affects the confidentiality, integrity or availability of the project, including related dependencies and services.

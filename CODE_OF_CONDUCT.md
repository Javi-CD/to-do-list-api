# Collaborator Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We, as members, taxpayers and leaders, commit ourselves to make participation in our project and our community a free experience for all, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sexual characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio -economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or identity and sexual orientation.
We are committed to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, cozy, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

---

## Our standards

Behavior examples that contribute to a positive environment for our community include:

-Demonstrate empathy and kindness to other people
-Respect opinions, views and different experiences
-Give and accept constructive feedback
-Assume responsibility and apologize to those who are affected by our mistakes, learning from experience
-Focus on what is better not only for us as individuals, but for the community in general

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or images, and sexual approaches or attention of any kind
- Derogative comments (trolling), insulting or repealing, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publish private information from other people, such as physical or email addresses, without your explicit permission
- Other behaviors that can be considered inappropriate in a professional environment

---

## Community responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our acceptable behavior standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective actions in response to any behavior that they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
Community leaders have the right and responsibility to eliminate, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki pages editions, ISSUES and other contributions that are not aligned with this code of conduct, and communicate the reasons for their decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This code of conduct is applied within all project spaces and also applies when a person officially represents the project or its community in public spaces.

---

## Application

Instances of abusive, stalker or unacceptable behavior can be reported to the leaders of the project responsible for the application in [Project contact mail]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated quickly and fairly.
All community leaders are obliged to respect the privacy and security of those who report incidents.
